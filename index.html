// 01 - Why Neocloud Summit

A NEW LAYER OF THE CLOUD
IS BEING BUILT IN REAL TIME.

Neoclouds sit between the chipmakers and data centers on one side and the model builders and enterprises on the other. The decisions being made now, on how they get built, financed, secured, and bought, will shape the next decade of AI. This is not a survey of AI in general. It's one focused day on the companies and decisions building the next cloud.

50+ Neoclouds in the room
250-300 Senior AI infrastructure leaders
30+ Speakers · one stage · one day

// Why now

Inference is outrunning capacity

Demand is scaling faster than supply, pulling a wave of new clouds into the market.

Power sets the pace

Power, land, and network are now the real limits on how fast anyone can build.

Capital is picking winners

Money is reshaping who wins, while the financing model for compute is still unsettled.

Enterprises are going to production

Pilots are becoming production, raising the bar on cost, security, and reliability.

The window to understand this market, and to be positioned in it, is open now.
